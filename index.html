I'm Rasha — a Ghanaian-born, Arlington-based Project Coordinator and Programs Manager with over 6 years of experience managing projects, teams, and programs across healthcare, logistics, and technology.

At Klicks Africa Foundation, I served as Programs Coordinator for four years — managing therapy program delivery, coordinating multidisciplinary teams, training staff, and leading projects including a full digital transformation of the centre's progress reporting system, moving from paper-based records to a paperless workflow using Google Forms, Google Sheets, and Excel.

I co-founded Errand Genie, a logistics and delivery startup in Ghana, where I coordinated operations and programs from the ground up. I have also been coordinating the Pebble project at Agrocenter since 2024 — a mobile agriculture platform serving Ghanaian farmers — using Jira and Slack in an Agile environment.

I am multilingual (English, Ga, Twi), a newly enlisted US Air Force Reservist, and deeply committed to using project coordination to drive meaningful impact in health-tech and community-focused organisations.
